## When it turns on

The project goes **read-only** as soon as you **preview a version**: you clicked a version's card
to look at it without committing anything. The content is presented exactly as it was captured.

## What you see

- A **read-only banner** across the full width, between the toolbar and the panels, naming the
  version you are viewing.
- A **Read only** pill where the unsaved-changes indicator usually sits.

## What you can and cannot do

- **You can** do anything that only reads: browse the panels and statistics, search, consult the
  agendas and computed times, and **export** (Help stays available too). An export then writes
  exactly what is on screen.
- **You cannot** write: editable fields, add and delete buttons, pickers simply disappear — they
  are not greyed out, they are withheld. Any half-started edit is cleared on entering the
  preview.

## How to leave

The banner offers two ways out:

- **Start from this version** — restore it, your way to branch from an older state;
- **Back to the current version** — leave the preview without changing anything.
