The vocabulary used in this guide and in the application.

**Scene** — a screenplay scene. In the French interface the same thing is called *une séquence*,
because the word *scène* names something else in that trade — a naming rule that touches French
alone.

**Fountain** — a plain-text screenplay format, the source of truth of a project. See [Fountain,
the source of truth](../concepts/fountain-source-of-truth.md).

**`.ocpt`** — a project's file. It holds everything: screenplay, breakdown, shot list, resources,
schedule, budget.

**`.ocptz`** — a project exported as a **portable package**, to pass to someone.

**Breakdown** — the pass where you tag, in the screenplay, everything the shoot must provide
(roles, sets, elements).

**Shot list** — the mode where you divide each scene into **shots**.

**Coverage** — the exact passages of the screenplay a shot films.

**Poste** — a budget heading of the French CNC nomenclature (for example *Transport, allowances,
on-set logistics*).

**CNC nomenclature** — the standard chart of accounts a French commission expects; the budget
creates its ten postes automatically.

**Commitment** — a spend ordered and owed, but not yet paid. It settles itself when the payments
reach it.

**Defrayal** — a reimbursement of costs a person paid out of pocket (meals, travel…).

**Slot** — a working unit within a shooting day, with its own location, crew and hours.

**Block** — a segment of a slot's timetable (a shot, a rehearsal, a meal, hair-and-make-up,
travel…).

**Call time** — the linking of a person to a slot, from which their arrival, **PAT** band and
departure are read.

**PAT** — *prêt à tourner*: the period when a person is costumed, made up and ready to shoot.

**HMC** — French for hair, make-up and costume (habillage, maquillage, coiffure).

**Call sheet** — the daily document handed to the crew and cast, produced by the schedule.

**Day Out of Days** — the cast schedule across the whole shoot, one row per role.

**Mise en scène** — in the shot list, the directing notes attached to a shot.
