# NNNN - Title in sentence case

## Status

Proposed

## Context

What forces this decision: the problem, the constraints, the alternatives on the table before a
choice was made. Facts, not opinions - a reader who disagrees with the decision should still
recognize this section as accurate.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated as a plain sentence. Enough concrete detail (types, file paths, exact
versions) that the decision is checkable against the code, not just a slogan.

## Consequences

What this costs, in addition to what it buys. Every decision has a downside; name it. Include
follow-up work it creates, and constraints it places on future changes.

## Alternatives considered

The other options that were on the table, and why each was not chosen. One line per alternative
is enough.
